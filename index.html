01

Her rhythm

The parts of the day that make Sadie feel like Sadie.

She is sweet in the morning, busy at night and still finding her rhythm with walks and toilet training.

MORNING NON-NEGOTIABLE

Cuddles. Lots of them.

She loves, loves cuddles in the morning. She gives tiny kisses on your ears and forehead, and when it is time to sleep, she curls up on your pillow. This is not a minor preference. It is how the day should begin.

EVENING ENERGY

Zoomies, play and a toy redirect

At night she gets rambunctious and wants to play. If she gets bitey, say “Where’s your toy?” or just “Toy”. She will go find one to play with.

WALKS

Build up slowly

She is not used to going out two or three times a day. Take it gently at first, then build up rather than changing her routine all at once.

TOILET REALITY

Pee pads help. Poo is still random.

She pees on pads, but she only wants to use a pad once or twice. After that, change it for a fresh one. Poo timing and location are not reliable yet, so keep an eye out and keep expectations kind.

02

Food, water & safety

The practical things that keep her comfortable and well.

Her stomach needs consistency, her water bowl needs attention and her harness needs a quick glance during big play.

MEALS

Three red cups a day

She does not always eat as soon as food is put down. With other dogs around, give her a calm, separate eating spot and coordinate the setup rather than letting another dog reach her bowl.

WATER

Check and refill several times

She drinks a lot of water. Keep fresh water available and fill the bowl a few times throughout the day.

TREATS + WORDS SHE KNOWS

A few treats, plus the full performance

Only a few treats here and there. Do not let her eat other dogs’ food or extra food, because it can make her sick. When you do give her one, say “Shvie” for sit and “Around” for her to spin around. Other words and phrases she knows: “I love you”, “wuv you”, “kisses”, “toy”, “no bites”, and “huggie”. It is extremely cute.

HARNESS CHECK

Secure, with one playful exception

Her harness is secure, but if she plays in it or jumps on people, a leg can sometimes slip out. Watch the fit during excitement and pause to fix it. Inside the house she is used to not wearing the harness - please take it off after each walk.

One detail to settle before shared-dog mealtimesSadie can take time to start eating. Agree on a separate, supervised feeding spot so there is no pressure from other dogs and no one steals her meal.
